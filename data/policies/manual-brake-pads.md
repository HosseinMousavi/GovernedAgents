# Product Manual: Ceramic Brake Pads

## Fitment
Check the vehicle compatibility table on the product page; pads are vehicle-specific.

## Bedding-in
After installation perform 8-10 moderate stops from 50 km/h, then 2-3 harder stops from 80 km/h, avoiding a full stop. Allow to cool.

## Noise
Light squeal in the first 200 km is normal. Persistent grinding indicates incorrect installation or worn rotors.

## Returns and warranty
Installed pads cannot be returned under the standard policy. Defective pads are covered for 24 months; a service appointment is required for inspection.
