# Product Manual: Dash Cam 2K

## Setup
Insert a Class 10 microSD card (32-256 GB), mount on the windshield behind the mirror, route the cable along the headliner to the 12V socket.

## Recording modes
Loop recording (default, 3-minute clips), event lock on impact (G-sensor), optional parking mode (requires the hardwire kit).

## Troubleshooting
- **No recording**: format the card in the camera's menu.
- **Blurry at night**: clean the lens and remove the shipping protector film.
- **Overheating warning**: park in shade; the camera pauses above 65 C and resumes automatically.

## Warranty
24 months. Water damage and card failures are not covered.
