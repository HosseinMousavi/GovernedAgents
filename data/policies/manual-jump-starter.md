# Product Manual: Jump Starter 2000A

## Safety
Never connect clamps to each other. Connect red to battery positive, black to a bare metal ground. Do not use on batteries below -20 C or above 60 C.

## Jump start
1. Charge the unit to at least 50%.
2. Connect clamps; wait for the green indicator.
3. Start the engine within 30 seconds. Wait 60 seconds between attempts, maximum 3 attempts.

## Maintenance
Recharge every 3 months in storage.

## Warranty
24 months. Covers cell failure and clamp defects. Does not cover reverse-polarity damage.
