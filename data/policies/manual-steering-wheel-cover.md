# Product Manual: Steering Wheel Cover (Leather / Microfiber)

## Fitment
Fits steering wheels 14.5 to 15.5 inches in outer diameter. Measure across the widest point before ordering.

## Installation
1. Warm the cover in sunlight or with a hair dryer for 2-3 minutes to soften it.
2. Position the seam at the bottom (6 o'clock).
3. Stretch over the top of the wheel, then work down both sides.
4. Leave in a warm car for 24 hours to set.

## Care
Wipe with a damp cloth. Do not use solvent cleaners on leather.

## Common issues
- **Cover will not stretch over the wheel**: the wheel is larger than 15.5 in; return under the 30-day policy.
- **Cover arrived torn, scuffed, or with split stitching**: this is damaged on arrival; report within 7 days for a free replacement or full refund.
- **Slipping**: ensure the inner grip ring faces the wheel.
