# Return & Refund Policy

## Eligibility window
Most items may be returned within **30 days of delivery** for a full refund to the original payment method. Maintenance fluids (oil, coolant, washer fluid) and opened care products are final sale.

## Damaged or defective on arrival
If an item **arrives damaged or defective**, report it within **7 days of delivery**. Damaged-on-arrival claims are eligible for a **full refund or free replacement**, and the customer does **not** pay return shipping. A photo of the damage may be requested. The support agent files a ticket with category `damaged`, and a refund over $0 requires manager approval (see Refund Approval Policy).

## Condition requirements
Items returned under the standard 30-day window must be unused, in original packaging, with all accessories. Installed parts (brake pads, rotors, belts) cannot be returned once installed unless defective.

## Refund timing
Approved refunds post within 5-7 business days. Store credit is immediate.

## Exchanges
Exchanges follow the same window as returns. Price differences are charged or refunded at exchange time.
