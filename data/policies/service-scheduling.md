# Service Scheduling

## Who can schedule
Service appointments are booked by **staff** on the customer's behalf. Customers request an appointment through support; the agent proposes a date and staff confirm it.

## Availability
Installation bays operate Monday-Saturday, 8:00-18:00. Appointments are 60 minutes for accessories, 120 minutes for brakes and batteries.

## Rescheduling and no-shows
Reschedule at least 24 hours ahead. Two consecutive no-shows require prepayment for the next booking.

## Warranty service
Warranty service is free of labor charges when the warranty check confirms coverage.
