# Shipping Policy

## Methods and timing
- Standard (free over $49): 3-6 business days.
- Expedited: 2 business days.
- Freight (running boards, tonneau covers, racks): 5-10 business days, curbside delivery.

## Tracking
A tracking number is emailed when the order status changes to `shipped`. Orders in `placed` status have not left the warehouse and can still be cancelled.

## Lost packages
If tracking shows delivered but the package is missing, wait 48 hours, check with neighbors, then contact support. We file a carrier claim and ship a replacement or refund after the carrier investigation (typically 3-5 business days).

## Address changes
Addresses can be changed while the order is `placed`. Once `shipped`, contact the carrier directly.
