# Warranty Policy

## Coverage periods
Each product lists a warranty period in months on its product page and on the order receipt. Typical periods:
- Interior accessories (covers, mats, organizers): 12 months
- Electronics (dash cams, scanners, chargers): 12-24 months
- Exterior hardware (racks, hitches, running boards): 36 months
- Consumables (fluids, filters, towels): no warranty beyond damaged-on-arrival protection

## What is covered
Manufacturing defects and premature failure under normal use.

## What is not covered
Wear items, damage from improper installation, accidents, modification, or commercial use.

## How to claim
Use the warranty check tool with your order number. If covered, a service appointment or replacement is arranged. Warranty claims do not require manager approval unless a cash refund is issued instead of replacement.
